# Motivation

Adam Grant: many people have friends and still feel lonely. Networks used to look like spider webs — the people in your life knew each other. Now they look like bicycle wheels: you are the hub, and the spokes never meet. Bonding is not the same as belonging.

Happiness rises and loneliness falls when you get involved in a group with a shared activity, or when you introduce friends to each other. A book club, a class, a league. The point is a community that can take care of each other when you are not in the room.

Grant’s ask is small: stop collecting one-to-one ties and start connecting the people you already have.
