# Motivation

Friday Forward #549 (Robert Glazer), “Beyond Reproach,” is a useful Monday filter for anyone whose work looks like open comments and easy Slack. Cheap questions are abundant. The rooms that matter are the ones where asking them has a price — a paper, a series, a model, a workplace — because dissent there can slow the schedule or bruise a reputation.

The useful cut is not “be braver in the abstract.” It is noticing which of your projects still punish the question, and putting the scarce ones there. AI makes it cheaper to produce a draft; it does not make it cheaper to own the judgment that survives contact with a real room. That is the same job Clara Ma is describing for chiefs of staff: sequencing and decision quality that a model will not sign.

If everything in the inbox is frictionless to challenge, you are probably not in the room that matters. Pick one place this week where a better question would actually cost you something, and ask it there.
