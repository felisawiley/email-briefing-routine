# Motivation

The quiet risk with AI is not that it answers for you. It is that a fluent answer arrives with the criteria already attached, and the part of you that would have argued with a bad suggestion never gets used. Asking a model what class to take is not like spellcheck. Spellcheck is wrong or right. A recommendation quietly supplies the standard you would have used to judge the list.

That standard is a muscle. It does not announce when it is gone. It just gets quieter each time you accept a ranked list instead of sitting with what you actually want. Assistance and abdication look the same from the inside. You still make a choice. It still looks like yours. The wanting may have been outsourced a few prompts back.

The useful test is whether the tool is touching how you execute a decision you already made, or what matters in the first place. Grammar sits near the line. Career and taste sit well on the wrong side of it. If you cannot remember the last time you disagreed with an answer you were given, that is the tell.
