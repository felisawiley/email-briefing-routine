The S&P 500 still won the growth contest — $1 became $35.90 in real terms, versus $12.89 in gold — but it did it with a 48% drawdown in 1974 against the “optimal” mix’s 13% in 2022. Maggiulli’s point is not to copy the weights. Cut the sample in half, stop in 1998, and homes vanish from the optimal mix entirely. The perfect portfolio is the solution to a problem that no longer exists.
