Edelman and Nielsen, in his telling, have been saying the same thing for a decade: paid ads keep losing, recommendations from a person you already follow keep winning, and trust has moved from the billboard to a smaller circle.

Founders, he says, keep reversing the order. Polish the logo, tighten the LinkedIn voice, buy attention, then wonder why nobody converts. The mechanism he wants instead is specific and public: what actually went wrong with the first customer, what you almost did not survive, shown up on a schedule people can set a watch to. One honest post is not an asset. The same honest voice every week is.

That is yesterday’s Maggiulli note and Tuesday’s E12, pointed at PausePoint and the Substack instead of the S&P. You do not need Shetty’s deal. You need the same sequence he used before anyone would pay for the voice: belief first, funnel second. Nobody buys from a stranger, and a second meter that can log into everything will not fix that.
