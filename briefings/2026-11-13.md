# Tool Updates

- **ChatGPT custom GPTs:** They retire December 11. Use a Project when you want chats, files, and instructions kept together; migrate a GPT to a plugin only if other people still need that same reusable tool. Old conversations stay.
- **Claude Meetings:** Use it when Claude should sit in a Google Meet, take notes, and start a doc or whiteboard while you are still talking. Team and Enterprise beta, Google Meet only for now.

# Motivation

We spend a lot of time asking what AI might become. Katelyn Jones's point is the quieter one: what we become in the hours we spend talking to it when nobody else is in the room.

Hannah Arendt split time alone into three kinds. Loneliness is being deserted — one voice, no community, nothing civic to think toward. Isolation is worse: you cannot add anything of your own to a shared world, so judgment collapses into whatever frame is handed to you. Solitude is the useful one. You are physically alone, but the inner conversation includes other people's views, and you come back able to act with them.

A chat thread can fake the first and dress it up as the third. It answers. It stays. It never needs you to go find a colleague. That is companionship that still leaves you separate, and certainty that still is not yours. Heavy use without a human check dulls the muscle that argues.

Use the model as a second voice you still argue with. Then close it and take the thought to a person. Solitude is a practice. Loneliness is what happens when the practice never ends.
