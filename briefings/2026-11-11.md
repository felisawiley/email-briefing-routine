Half of American men 18–49 now have an online sportsbook account, and legal handle went from $6.6 billion to $165 billion after the 2018 Supreme Court ruling. The house keeps more of each dollar by pushing parlays that pay worse than the true odds. Prediction markets then slipped the remaining state rules: federal commodities regulation, weaker age gates, no self-exclusion lists. The product is still sports betting, aimed at the group most likely to get hurt.

The money is the easy loss. Maggiulli’s grandfather gambled away more than a million dollars, then his marriage and the family that would have sat with him. Research already ties legal sports betting to bankruptcies and more domestic violence after upset losses. The CEOs talk about responsible play while pitching longshots as generational wealth. The old gambler at least had to drive to the track. Today it is a phone.

The point is not a lecture about willpower. It is that every remaining friction is being removed, and the cost that does not show up on the statement is the one that lasts.
