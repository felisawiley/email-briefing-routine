# Motivation

Nick Maggiulli’s *The Best Way to Sell a Concentrated Position* is a calm checklist for anyone sitting on RSUs, a single-stock winner, or — in his framing — SpaceX equity after a volatile IPO. The research is blunt: most IPOs underperform size-matched peers after lock-up, and the median individual stock lags the market over a decade. A handful of winners pull the average up; picking one name and holding forever is usually a wealth-destroying bet dressed as loyalty.

He walks through exits: sell everything (median-wealth maximizing), tranche for taxes, harvest losses via direct indexing, sell half to minimize regret, pre-commit to a schedule, or sell up to a “wealth level” that locks in a lifestyle floor and leave a moonshot. For a typical SpaceX employee with ~$100k of equity, tax gymnastics barely matter — diversify most of it. The harder truth for the rest of us: run the same math on your concentrated bet. You will usually be told to sell more than you want. You will not get filthy rich that way, Maggiulli writes — but you will also not go poor.
