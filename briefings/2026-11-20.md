# Motivation

How leaders act off the clock is not a sealed file. Adam Grant’s overnight note, written after another high-profile CEO resignation, is really about a pattern. Using the Ashley Madison leak, researchers found that CEOs, CFOs, police officers, and financial advisors who cheated on partners were more likely to break rules at work too. People who dodge personal taxes tend to run more aggressive corporate tax shops. Opportunism, entitlement, and greed do not stay in one room.

The slogan “how you do anything is how you do everything” is too neat. Plenty of people who are meticulous at work keep a messy apartment. We turn conscientiousness up where it matters to us. Grant’s cleaner version: how you do some things is often how you do other things. Someone who repeatedly crosses a line at home is not a different character at nine in the morning.

Integrity is not a nine-to-five job. Do not give leaders a pass because the scandal started after hours.
