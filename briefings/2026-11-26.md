# Motivation

A long to-do list trains the brain to hunt for what is still wrong — the unfinished thread, the thing you forgot. Daniela Wolfe’s note is that gratitude is not a mood you wait for. It is a short attention shift. You can be tired and still register a first sip of coffee. You can be worried and still keep ten quiet minutes that nobody needed from you.

The practice is small on purpose. At the end of the day, name three things you appreciated. Then sit with why they landed — supported, quiet, connected — long enough to feel it again, not just to tick a list. The point is not a prettier journal. It is ending the day on something other than the leftover pile.

You do not have to wait until everything is organized. Life is already happening in the ordinary moments that get skipped because the next item is louder.
